## Recorded prose

An endless stone spiral seen from just above its rim: a single road enters at the left, crosses the frame dead level, and then winds itself inward and downward through a stack of shrinking rings. Each ring is a wall of cut stone, thinner and darker than the last, and the last is barely wider than a coin. Nothing else is in the frame — no figure, no sky, no horizon. The whole picture is the fact that a road which runs one direction ends up going down.
