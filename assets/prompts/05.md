## Recorded prose

A single ordinary chair — four legs, a flat seat, a plain back — split down the middle by a hard vertical seam and mirrored so exactly that the two halves no longer agree. Left of the seam it is drawn one way; right of the seam the same chair is drawn slightly differently, and neither half is the odd one. The background is dense ink-work that also mirrors and also refuses to resolve. Keep looking and the thing keeps declining to become a single object.
