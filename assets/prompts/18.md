## Recorded prose

One large eye-form with four pupils instead of one, arranged two by two, each pupil showing the same scene in a different horizontal band of colour: through one you see it in red, through the next in orange, the next in blue, the last in a fourth band. Four passes of the same vision at once. The eye is otherwise beautifully and slightly absurdly drawn. More eyes means more perception, and the picture has to feel like a cost rather than a gain.
