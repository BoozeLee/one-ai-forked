Between them, where the two almost touch, a black shape with no grain in it: a head at the top, shoulders, and a coat spreading down off the bottom edge. ## Recorded prose

A ring of light, bright and hard edged, and inside it a hole of absolutely pure black shaped like a person standing. The black is not dark grey and not shadow — it has no texture at all, nothing for the eye to catch on — and the ring around it is the only thing in the frame with any structure. The void is where a second person should be. It is the loudest shape available and it is drawn with the least material.
