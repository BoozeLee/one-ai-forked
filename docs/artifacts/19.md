## Decode — what is visibly on the plate

Two mirrored horned figures facing each other across the centre, blue to the shoulder and burning pink above it, manes of long pink strands going up and out, a horn curling back from the top of each head. Four orange spiral eyes, the same spiral in all four. Between them, where the two almost touch, a black shape with no grain in it: a head at the top, shoulders, and a coat spreading down off the bottom edge. Higher, in the gap between the two horns, a second pure-black void, perfectly round, the size of a closed hand. Both voids carry the same thin cold cyan rim-light the figures have where they meet the air, and the rim closes over the gap between the head-shape and the round black without turning. Ground is near-black with magenta tendrils.
